The short version

No data leaves your device when you use the timeline tool. The dates and labels you type are processed only by code running in your browser. We do not receive them.

What stays on your device

  • If you tap “Save in this browser”, your entries are stored in your browser’s local storage on that device only. You can delete them at any time with “Delete all Mohlat data from this browser”, or by clearing your browser’s site data.
  • The calendar (.ics) file is created on your device and downloaded directly to it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up and no document uploads.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or cookies set by us.
  • The page’s security policy blocks the tool from sending network requests.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when it delivers the pages to you. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details. We do not receive any tool data through hosting.

If you email us

If you email us (for example to join founding-member reminders), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, such as a WhatsApp number or document dates. We use this only to reply and to send the reminders you ask for. We do not sell it. You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing us. Please do not send passport or visa scans.

Payments (future)

If we offer paid plans later, payments will be handled by a payment provider, and this policy will be updated before any payment is taken to explain what data that provider processes.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you (which is only what you have emailed us). Email shashankbhasin21@gmail.com.
